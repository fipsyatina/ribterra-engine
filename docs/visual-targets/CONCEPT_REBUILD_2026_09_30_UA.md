## What the Result Is

1. Every interactive new settlement has a new seed. The open `--seed`, loading and repeating the test remains fixed.
2. Rivers have different curves, narrow and wide places; natural rocky shores. No repeating straight line.
3. The hills, cliffs, slopes, and high cliffs are all visible in the usual opening scene. The movement, the movement of buildings and the selection of mice correspond to the geometry of the visible.
4. Forests, hedges, rocks, and berries form irregularly mixed areas, not rectangular plantations. The startup resources are available, there's a place to build.
5. Completely new art reproduced by pixel references: saturated green pine trees, round blue-grey rocks, earth light, blue water, wood/stone/sunny roofs, warm windows. The old set is not an artistic orientation.
6. HUD corresponds to the composition of the concept: decorative gold edges, dark blue flooring, large painted icons, readable antique font, object bottom card, four central actions, block of card/time. The weather data and the mini-map must be real, not drawn with sound figures.
7. The functionality V01–V03 is retained, the repeating F1 hides/reverses the entire HUD, the tracks accumulate from the actual movement, the old preservation is not regenerated by the hidden.
8. The reception takes place behind the actual frames of the game being played, next to the relevant concept, checking each item. The number of tests does not replace this test.
