# Ribterra art direction

The eleven images extracted from the author's DOCX are AI-generated concept illustrations and UI mockups. Preserve them as references. They are not in-game screenshots, production sprite sheets, or evidence of implemented systems.

Use the visual vocabulary in these references: fixed isometric miniature worlds; cold navy and muted blue; fine warm gold UI borders; timber, slate and stone; amber windows and firelight; readable people among buildings and conifers. Preserve hierarchy between terrain, resource nodes, structures, people and UI.

M0 deliberately uses original procedural geometry rendered by the client. It provides real camera, depth sorting, unit movement and placement validation on a tile map. Final art fidelity requires an asset production phase; do not substitute concept wallpaper for interactive gameplay.

Next art tasks: compare 64×32 and 128×64 tile production scales; define common anchors/footprints; create one house, worker, conifer, rock, farm and resource icon sheet; define atlas padding and depth metadata; validate silhouettes at minimum zoom. Add season variants and lighting only after the first set is readable at 1080p and compact window sizes.

Original concepts remain in `docs/source/concepts`. Actual prototype captures belong to `artifacts/` locally or selected documented captures in `docs/screenshots`, clearly labeled M0. Only genuine gameplay captures may be used as gameplay evidence.
