# Ribterra 0.25.9 — Management of production and workers

> English rendering of a historical project document, translated offline and checked for preservation of code, paths, identifiers, numeric literals, and recorded status markers. Historical records apply to their named snapshots and do not validate the current build. For exact original wording, see import commit `7388a2f`.

In the enterprise inspector, select a recipe, **1–20 parties**, an employee, and press «Start». For example, two sets of rains need 6 wood and give 12 rains. Raw materials and output of each batch are shown separately from the order result.

The automatic selection takes a free adult without a profession or permanent appointment. The occupant can be selected from the list to read the reason: constant work, production, manual command, transfer, expedition, path finding or control by another player. To work continuously, click "Manage this worker" and clearly stop it in the available occupations panel. After the current delivery or route of the inhabitant is completed, the appointment can be made manually. Profession, job and priorities are maintained; The automatic operation remains off until the next obvious destination.

The order no longer wipes out the profession and takes away the farmer's appointment. The server rejects incompatible simultaneous work. A group order shows the result for each construct: accepted, awaiting confirmation, or omitted for a reason. Repeated structures are not duplicated; The employee is used only once within the group. If a particular person is selected, it serves only one group structure.

Cancellation shall also be accepted during the transfer. The **«Finishes delivery[** means that the cargo is still in hand; The next party won't start. After delivery, the worker is dismissed. Unused raw materials of the enterprise and spent materials of the unfinished batch are returned to the ground for normal transport. The batch already made will not reimburse the raw material a second time. If the delivery is broken, the physical goods remain on the ground. To reach the object limit of returning two ingredients is deferred entirely — repeated attempts do not duplicate the first ingredient.

The request for cancellation is being kept with the world. The shipment continues the same delivery and dismissal of the worker. The format of the safe and network protocol is unchanged; The version of the rules is up to 15. The 0.25.8 reservation is maintained. For a shared game, all clients and servers must use 0.25.9.

New professions, recipes, lines or general redesign are not added. Ownership of independent nations remains a separate business 0.25.10.
