# Ribterra 0.25.1 — Lobbying the concept and boots

> English rendering of a historical project document, translated offline and checked for preservation of code, paths, identifiers, numeric literals, and recorded status markers. Historical records apply to their named snapshots and do not validate the current build. For exact original wording, see import commit `7388a2f`.

The lobby is designed as a meeting room: portrait cards, a background with flags and candles, a valley illustration, world rules, and the bottom of the readiness and launch buttons. The valley illustration is an artwork, not a picture of a generated map. Support for 2–6 seats is maintained; In the form of creation, four were originally selected.

On the free card, the master presses Add bot». The bot takes its seat, receives a separate command, and automatically is ready. The master can change his command or press "Free space". The rest of the places remain open to the public. Empty spaces do not block the start: you can start alone or with bots. You can't change your boots after launch.

Different teams — separate settlements. The bot, on its own, begins building a house and farm and gives free residents limited orders to deliver food, wood and stone. Extraction and delivery are done on the map through a regular delivery system. It's basic economic management, without diplomacy or war.

If a bot is in a human crew, it only controls the inhabitants it is clearly entrusted with. He doesn't start to spend the resources on building on a team like this. Human commands and active assignments are not overwritten. A new manual command can take control of the bot.

Boots and teams are kept together with the world and restored as they continue. The pause also stops the bot control. Passwords, direct connections, chat and overlays from the previous release are saved. All participants use 0.25.1, the network protocol 19.

The "Address friends" button copies the connection address. It can be shared with friends for entry via online games. It's a direct connection; Steam invitation integration remains a separate submission.
